Ground floor 1

Region

A city where AWS has data centers, like Singapore (ap-southeast-1). You pick one for speed, data laws and price.

Region = the city.

Keywords: data sovereignty, latency, "closest to customers"
Ground floor 2

Availability Zone

Separate data-center buildings inside a Region, each with its own power and network. Put your app in 2+ AZs so one blackout doesn't take you down.

AZ = separate buildings. Multi-AZ = don't put all your eggs in one building.

Keywords: high availability, fault tolerance, Multi-AZ
Ground floor 3

Edge Location

Small outposts close to users. CloudFront and Route 53 use them to answer fast without going back to the Region.

Edge = a convenience store on every corner.

Keywords: caching, CDN, low latency for global users
